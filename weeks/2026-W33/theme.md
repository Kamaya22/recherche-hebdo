# Week 2026-W33 — The deep ocean

**Theme:** The deep ocean — what we know about Earth's least-explored biome and why it matters for climate and biodiversity

## Why it matters

The deep ocean — broadly defined as waters below 200 m, spanning the bathyal, abyssal, and hadal zones down to nearly 11 km — covers more than 95% of the ocean's volume and roughly 65% of Earth's surface, yet remains far less surveyed than the surface of Mars. It drives global climate by storing vast quantities of carbon and heat, and anchors the thermohaline circulation that distributes energy around the planet. Its extraordinary biodiversity, shaped by darkness, crushing pressure, and near-freezing temperatures, has overturned assumptions about the limits of life and provided templates for biotechnology. Accelerating threats — deep-sea mining, plastic pollution reaching hadal trenches, and climate-driven deoxygenation — make understanding this biome not merely a scientific curiosity but an urgent conservation and governance imperative.

## Tentative 7-day outline

- **Day 1 — Panorama:** What counts as "the deep ocean"? The major depth zones, why the deep is so poorly explored, and a bird's-eye view of why it matters for Earth systems.
- **Day 2 — Physical environment & life's adaptations:** Pressure, temperature, total darkness, scarce nutrients; chemosynthesis at hydrothermal vents and cold seeps; the adaptations that allow animals to survive at extreme depths.
- **Day 3 — Biodiversity:** What lives there — the surprising richness of abyssal and hadal fauna, bioluminescence, the biological pump linking surface productivity to the deep.
- **Day 4 — Deep ocean and climate:** Carbon sequestration, the biological and solubility pumps, thermohaline circulation and deep water formation, the role of the deep in moderating climate change.
- **Day 5 — Threats:** Deep-sea mining (polymetallic nodules, hydrothermal vent fields), plastic and chemical pollution at extreme depth, climate change (warming, deoxygenation, acidification).
- **Day 6 — Exploration technology:** ROVs, AUVs, landers, Argo floats, acoustic methods; what we can and cannot measure; the funding and geopolitical context of ocean exploration.
- **Day 7 — Synthesis:** What is settled vs. open; the biggest knowledge gaps; governance challenges; 3–5 open questions and pointers for going deeper.
